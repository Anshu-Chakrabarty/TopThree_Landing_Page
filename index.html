About Us

Who we are &
how we rank agents

Top 3 in Realty is an independent agent-matching platform founded to bring transparency to the real estate industry. We are not a brokerage, and we do not represent buyers or sellers directly.

Our platform has helped over 1.8 million property sellers connect with verified local listing agents since our founding. That figure is based on the cumulative number of lead submissions processed through our platform.

We earn revenue through referral fees paid by agents when a successful match is made. This does not influence our rankings — all agent rankings are determined independently of referral arrangements.

Our 50-Point Vetting Methodology

  • ✓ Active state real estate license verification
  • ✓ MLS transaction history & verified sales volume
  • ✓ Days-on-market performance vs. local average
  • ✓ List-price-to-sale-price ratio analysis
  • ✓ Client review quality & volume assessment
  • ✓ Disciplinary & complaint record check
  • ✓ Local market specialization & property type fit
  • ✓ Re-verified every 6 months — no legacy rankings

No agent can pay to improve their ranking. Rankings reflect performance data only.

Testimonial disclaimer: Agent matches and outcomes displayed represent individual experiences. Results may vary depending on market conditions, property type, and agent availability.
